About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I work with Prof. Sangtae Ha in the Internet Systems Lab. My research is on networked systems for real-time media: RDMA- and DPU-accelerated infrastructure for high-throughput, low-latency streaming and AI systems, and multipath 5G transport for high-quality video calls at large scale. Recent first-author work includes eXpressSFU (NSDI '26), which scales video conferencing with SmartNICs, and DeepSFU (SIGCOMM '26), scalable deepfake detection for video conferencing.

Before Boulder, I spent three years as a research engineer at Singapore Management University working on multimodal human–robot instruction comprehension and low-power pervasive vision. I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, graduating first in my major with a thesis on FPGA-based intrusion detection for software-defined networking. In summer 2026 I was a student researcher on the Multimedia Streaming team at ByteDance.
